Why these charts replace the current ones
  1. Current per-alternative chart plots undiscounted and discounted bars side by side on positions 1 to 31 with no year labels, and its title is set once by VBA and goes stale when alternatives are reordered. The two stream panels above share one calendar axis and one scale.
  2. Current Summary chart is a clustered bar of initial cost versus present worth with a raw-number axis. It cannot show why one alternative wins. The bridge does that in one read.
  3. Nothing today shows robustness. The sensitivity line and the tornado answer the reviewer's first question: does this hold at 7 percent, and what happens if salvage is wrong.
  4. Nothing today shows closures, which is the whole reason the 2026 update exists.
