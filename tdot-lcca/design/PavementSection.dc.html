Concrete is the one material whose pay item names its own thickness.
How each thickness is read back.  Volume items: inches = C.Y. × 27 × 12 / (mainline S.Y. × 9). Asphalt by weight: inches = TON × 2000 / 145 pcf × 12 / (mainline S.Y. × 9). Items measured by area carry no implied thickness.
Checked against the completed runs.  Murfreesboro's quantities read back as 5.00″ P-401 on 16.00″ P-209, exactly the section typed into its Alternative Description, and 145 pcf is the unit weight that reproduces it to the hundredth of an inch.
Worth a look: the Murfreesboro concrete alternative is described as 8″ P-501 but priced on the 9-inch pay item. There is no 8-inch item in the list, so the estimate carries 9″. A read-back like this one would have caught it.
