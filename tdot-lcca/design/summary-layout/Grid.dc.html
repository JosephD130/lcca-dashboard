Four findings
What the first screen is spending its height and width on.
Two rows of three tiles cost 169 px for six numbers.
One flush row of six does the same job in 60 px.
−109 px
A section title and a how-to line sit between the verdict and the first plot.
Both belong in the header band or a cell note, not in the first screen.
−64 px
Every plot is inset 10 px from its neighbour.
Three gutters across the supporting row is 30 px of grey between instruments; a 1 px hairline reads as one panel and gives the width back to the plots.
−29 px
The band is 1,239 px on a screen that offers 1,488.
The 249 px to the right of column R is empty — almost exactly the width the project location card needs.
