LCCA workbook — design system
One band, one header, one card, one type scale across every sheet
The rule that governs everything: one 1,240 px band
Nothing sits to the right of the band. Reference blocks that used to live off-screen (the locator map, the at-a-glance facts) move below the charts. Helper data stays far right, outside the print area, where nobody scrolls to it. The sheet scrolls vertically only.
