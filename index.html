Methodology, sources, and simplifications
  • Agency cost NPV: each activity's construction cost is discounted from its year of action; annual maintenance costs are discounted year-by-year over the activity's life within the analysis period. Remaining-service-life credit (when enabled) prorates the last activity's cost by its unused life at the end of the analysis period and credits it as a negative cost in the final year — the RealCost 2.5 convention.
  • User costs follow the FHWA "calculated" work-zone method: hourly demand (AADT × hourly distribution × directional split) is compared with work-zone capacity during closure hours. Delay components: work-zone traversal delay (reduced speed over the work-zone length), speed-change delay and vehicle operating cost (FHWA Aug-1996 added-time/added-cost tables, CPI-escalated to 2012 as in RealCost 2.5.7CA), queue delay from a deterministic queuing diagram, and idling VOC on queued vehicle-hours. Delay is monetized per vehicle class with the values of time above.
  • Hourly distributions are the RealCost built-ins (MicroBENCOST rural/urban, Weekday Double-Peak) plus the custom distribution found in the source workbook's traffic_c sheet, including directional splits by hour.
  • Traffic growth compounds annually from the construction year and is capped at Maximum AADT when provided.
  • Seeded from RealCostV2.5.7CA 64bit.xlsm: analysis options, values of time, the four alternative M&R sequences (names, years of action, service lives, maintenance costs). Costs and work-zone details that were blank in the workbook default to zero and must be entered for a meaningful comparison.
  • Simplifications vs full RealCost 2.5.7CA: deterministic only (no Monte Carlo simulation); no COZEEP/TMP cost modules; free-flow speed taken as the posted speed limit; queue speed handled through the deterministic queuing diagram rather than the forced-flow speed curve; work-zone closures assumed on all work-zone days.
